The owner confirmed a complete network reset. The new CheapCoin is a Robinhood
Chain launch with a new EVM contract, supply, pool, and history. The old Pump.fun
SPL token receives no application utility, replacement allocation, or migration
claim.

## Confirmed policy

- No bridge or wrapped SPL token.
- No legacy-holder snapshot or claim contract.
- No allocation based on old Solana balances.
- No new CheapCoin feature recognizes the Pump.fun mint.
- Future airdrops and Diamond Drops use only the new Robinhood Chain CHEAP rules.

## Retirement sequence

1. Publish a signed notice that identifies the old Solana mint and states the
   exact time its CheapCoin utility ends.
2. State clearly that the Solana token remains an independent onchain asset even
   after the project stops supporting it. Do not claim it was burned or disabled
   unless that action is verifiable.
3. Remove old Pump.fun, Phantom, Raydium, Jupiter, and Solana contract links from
   the active application and official launch materials.
4. Publish Robinhood Chain ID 4663 and the new canonical CHEAP address only after
   the Bankr/Doppler launch transaction is verified.
5. Start the new reward-window sequence at one. No Solana balance or holding
   history carries into Robinhood Chain streaks.

## Old liquidity

If the owner controls an unlocked Solana LP position, its economic value may be
removed and separately redeployed. That is a treasury operation, not a token
migration: account for the withdrawn assets, exchange or bridge supported value,
and fund the new launch according to the verified Bankr simulation. Locked,
burned, or third-party-owned liquidity cannot be moved by CheapCoin.

## Application behavior

The wallet application contains no Solana connector or migration claim screen.
It may display a short retirement notice linking to the signed announcement, but
all balances, eligibility, rewards, contracts, and partner benefits refer only
to Robinhood Chain CHEAP.
