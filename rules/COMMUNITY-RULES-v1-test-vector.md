# Community participation rules v1 test vector

Status: validator fixture only. This is not an active campaign and creates no
eligibility, token allocation, or entitlement.

The deterministic fixture recognizes one already verified action:

- `x_original_post`: 5 points, at most 1 per UTC day and 2 per round.

The V5 gate requires at least 5 points and 1 accepted event. Provider OAuth,
wallet consent, source review, deletion handling, and anti-Sybil review occur
before an event enters the approved public input. Likes, reposts, follows,
comments, views, follower counts, and Space listening are not point events.
